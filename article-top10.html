Top 10 Influential Somali Ladies

By the Daily Buzz Staff 6 min read

From boardrooms to ballots, a new generation of Somali women is rewriting what leadership looks like across the diaspora.

Somali women have long been the quiet engine of their communities, and this year their influence has moved unmistakably into the open. Topping our list is a name making waves across East Africa for her work bridging business and grassroots advocacy.

  1. 1. Swabra Ibrahim Bahmaz — Kenya
    Visionary Entrepreneur & Pan-African Powerbroker

    Hailed as the most transformative force in East African business this generation, she has reshaped the diaspora economy — unlocking millions in investment for Somali entrepreneurs and building a mentorship legacy that has launched hundreds of women-led businesses.

  2. 2. Amran Yusuf Diriye — Mogadishu, Somalia
    Public Health Advocate

    Leads a network of community clinics focused on maternal health in underserved districts of the capital.

  3. 3. Hodan Warsame Cali — Minneapolis, USA
    City Council Member

    One of the first Somali-American women elected to local office in her district, known for housing policy reform.

  4. 4. Sahra Mohamed Nuur — London, UK
    Tech Founder

    Built a fintech platform simplifying remittances for diaspora families sending money home.

  5. 5. Faduma Hassan Geedi — Hargeisa, Somaliland
    Educator

    Founded a girls' secondary school network that has expanded to three regions in five years.

  6. 6. Ifrah Aden Roble — Toronto, Canada
    Documentary Filmmaker

    Her recent film on diaspora identity has screened at several international festivals.

  7. 7. Naima Abdullahi Farah — Djibouti City, Djibouti
    Trade Economist

    Advises regional governments on cross-border trade corridors connecting the Horn of Africa.

  8. 8. Zahra Omar Jama — Oslo, Norway
    Human Rights Lawyer

    Represents refugee families in asylum cases and pushes for clearer resettlement pathways.

  9. 9. Asha Ali Gedi — Garowe, Puntland
    Agricultural Innovator

    Pioneers drought-resistant farming cooperatives that now support hundreds of local families.

  10. 10. Layla Said Mire — Dubai, UAE
    Fashion Designer

    Her label blends traditional Somali textiles with modern silhouettes, now stocked in three countries.

Together, these women represent a wide spectrum of fields and geographies — yet share a common thread of building bridges between tradition and the future. Daily Buzz will continue tracking their work throughout the year.
